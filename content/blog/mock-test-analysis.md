## A score is only the starting point

The purpose of analysis is to decide what to change before your next attempt. Record patterns rather than reacting to one result.

## Review accuracy and selection

Check every incorrect and unattempted question. Ask whether the problem was knowledge, calculation, interpretation, time or question selection.

## Review time by section

Look for questions that consumed disproportionate time. Practise recognising when to move on.

## Create the next action

End the review with a short list: topics to revise, question types to practise and one test-taking behaviour to improve.
