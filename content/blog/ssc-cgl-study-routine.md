## Choose consistency over intensity

A routine that fits work, college and rest is more useful than an ambitious schedule you cannot repeat.

## Plan by outcomes

Define what you will finish in a session: a topic, a question set or a mock review. Add buffer time for difficult areas.

## Measure weekly

Compare accuracy, completion time and topic coverage each week. Adjust the next week based on evidence rather than anxiety.
