## Start with the current notification

Before making a timetable, read the latest official IBPS notification. Confirm the stages, subjects, marking rules and eligibility that apply to your cycle.

## Use three preparation blocks

A balanced week can include **concept learning**, **timed sectional practice**, and **full mock analysis**. Avoid taking mock tests without reviewing why an answer was wrong or slow.

### Build concepts

Identify weak topics through a short diagnostic test. Study one small topic at a time and follow it with focused questions.

### Practise under time pressure

Sectional practice helps you decide which questions to attempt first. Track accuracy as well as speed.

### Review every mock

Classify errors as concept gaps, calculation errors, reading errors or poor question selection. Use that list to plan the next week.

## Keep the plan adjustable

Your timetable should respond to performance. Give more time to recurring weaknesses without neglecting stronger sections.
